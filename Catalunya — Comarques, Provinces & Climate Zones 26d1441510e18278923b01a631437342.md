# Catalunya — Comarques, Provinces & Climate Zones

> Catalunya is divided into **42 comarques** (+ Aran, a unique territorial entity) grouped within **4 provinces**.
Climate zones follow the classification used by Catalan and Spanish meteorological research:
Mediterranean Coastal · Mediterranean Pre-Coastal · Humid Subtropical · Mediterranean Continental ·
Semi-arid/Steppe · Pyrenean Mediterranean · Alpine/High Mountain · Atlantic/Oceanic.
> 

---

## Climate Zone Legend

| Symbol | Climate Zone | Characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🌊 | **Mediterranean Coastal** | Mild winters, hot dry summers, autumn rain peaks, strong sea influence |
| 🏘️ | **Mediterranean Pre-Coastal** | Slightly more extreme than coast, less maritime dampening |
| 🌿 | **Humid Subtropical** | Warm/wet, no real dry season, transitional between Mediterranean and oceanic |
| 🏔️ | **Mediterranean Continental** | Cold winters, very hot summers, low rainfall, inland character |
| 🌵 | **Semi-arid / Steppe** | Very low rainfall (<400 mm/yr), extreme heat, sparse vegetation |
| ⛰️ | **Pyrenean Mediterranean** | Cool summers, cold winters, higher precipitation, transitional mountain |
| ❄️ | **Alpine / High Mountain** | Snow-rich winters, short cool summers, temps near 0°C annually |
| 🌧️ | **Atlantic / Oceanic** | Mild all year, distributed rainfall, green landscape — unique to Aran |
